Definition 3.5. Let $M$ be a smooth manifold endowed with a connection $\nabla$. A curve

$\gamma : [a, b] \to M$ is said to be autoparallel and is called a geodesic of $(M, \nabla)$ if it satisfies

for all $t \in [a, b]$

$$ \nabla_{\dot{\gamma}(t)} \dot{\gamma}(t) = 0.$$ (3.6)
