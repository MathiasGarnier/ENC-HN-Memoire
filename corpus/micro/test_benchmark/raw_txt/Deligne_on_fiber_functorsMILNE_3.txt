as all $\omega'$ (resp all $\omega^{''}$) are isomorphic, it suffices

Indeed $\tau_1$ and $\tau_2$ differ by an automorphism of $\omega' | \mathcal{T}' \cap \mathcal{T}^{''}$,

and such an automorphism lifts to an automorphism of $\omega'$ :

$$ G/A (k) \to G/ AB (k) \quad \quad \text{ is onto.}$$

We hence have here categories with just one 

isomorphism class of objects, and the question is to 

compare automorphism group. We need to check 

$$ G \xrightarrow{\sim} \{ (g', g^{''}) \in G/A, G/B | g' \textnormal{ and } g^{''} \textnormal{ have the same image in } G/AB \} $$

$$\begin{array}{ccc}
    G & \longrightarrow & G/A \
    \downarrow & \square & \downarrow \
    G/B & \longrightarrow & G/AB
\end{array}$$

bijectivity of this morphism of groups amounts to $A \cap B = \{ 0\}$,

surjectivity : if $\overline{g}'$, $\overline{g}^{''}$ lift $g'$, $g^{''}$,
